# LinkedIn Post Creator

Create engaging, authentic LinkedIn posts that drive discussion without generic corporate fluff.

## When to Use

- The user asks to write, draft, generate, or polish a LinkedIn post.
- The user shares notes, a project milestone, an article, or an opinion and wants to turn it into a post.
- The user asks for hooks, formats, or angles for LinkedIn content.

## Steps

1. Clarify the core insight, target audience, and key takeaway if they are not already obvious from the user's input.
2. Structure the post for LinkedIn readability:
   - Strong hook: An intriguing first line (before the "see more" cut-off) that establishes tension, curiosity, or value.
   - Body: Clean spacing with short paragraphs. Share practical context, narrative tension, or specific lessons.
   - Takeaway / Call to Action: A concluding thought or question that invites natural comments and discussion.
3. Offer 2 variations when appropriate:
   - Story / Narrative: Grounded in personal or professional experience with a clear lesson.
   - Direct / Actionable: Punchy breakdown of takeaways, frameworks, or observations.
4. Keep the voice grounded and human. Avoid excessive emojis, buzzword salad, and forced hustle cliches.

## Gotchas

- Do not use em dashes.
- Keep the line breaks natural rather than forcing every single sentence onto a new line unless deliberate emphasis is needed.
- Align tone with authentic professional sharing rather than superficial self-congratulation.
